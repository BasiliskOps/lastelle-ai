Method

Every engagement ends in working software that you own outright.

  1. I

    Survey

    I map your data, systems, and constraints, and agree on what success will be measured against.

  2. II

    Architect

    A reference design with the models, retrieval, orchestration, and controls chosen for your case.

  3. III

    Build

    Working software delivered in small, verifiable increments, each one tested against the agreed measures.

  4. IV

    Hand over

    Documentation, evaluation harnesses, and clear ownership, so the system keeps running without me.
