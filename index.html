Method

    Survey

  2. 02

FAQ

Questions that come up before a first call.

What kind of work does Annatar take on?

Solutions architecture and agentic systems for organizations putting language models into live operations, plus products released under the Annatar name.

Who does the work?

Damien Rosecraft, from the first call to production. Annatar is a one person practice, so nothing is handed off to a team you never met.

Which models and platforms are supported?

Systems are built across Claude, OpenAI, and xAI, with the model chosen for each job rather than one vendor for everything.

What do I own at the end of an engagement?

The working software, its documentation, and the evaluation harnesses that measure it. Ownership is clear from the start.

Where do people stay in the loop?

Human checkpoints are placed wherever a decision needs judgment, and agents handle the rest.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within two business days, with a first read on scope and architecture.
