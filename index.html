Mission

For the acceleration of the human project

Lastelle AI synthesizes solutions at the confluence of human judgment and machine intelligence, where intuition and conviction lend direction to computation on a scale no single mind could hold. Each system is composed with the deliberation of the workshop and carried out with the reach of the frontier, so that what leaves the bench extends what people are able to build, understand, and become.

Lastelle's long-term roadmap extends to robotics and unmanned systems for terrestrial and orbital applications.

FAQ

Common questions

What does Lastelle AI build?

Lastelle AI designs and builds agentic AI systems and AI-embedded hardware.

What is agentic AI?

An agent is autonomous software that acts on behalf of a person, team or enterprise. Agentic AI is one or more large language model agents acting autonomously in that role.

Does Lastelle AI take client work?

Lastelle builds its own products. Partnership inquiries are welcome.

What technology are the systems built on?

How quickly will I hear back?
